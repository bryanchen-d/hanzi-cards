This license covers the generated deck in [`public/js/lib/deck-data.js`](public/js/lib/deck-data.js) and the curated list in [`scripts/data/starter.tsv`](scripts/data/starter.tsv). The rest of the repository is code, which is not covered here.

### wordfreq (character frequency order)

The order of characters after the starter deck is derived from **wordfreq** by Robyn Speer et al., <https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq>. Its data is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The Chinese frequencies combine the following sources:

- **SUBTLEX-CH**: Cai, Q., & Brysbaert, M. (2010). *SUBTLEX-CH: Chinese word and character frequencies based on film subtitles.* PLoS One, 5(6), e10729. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0010729>. SUBTLEX is freely available data, at <http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies>.
- **Google Books Ngrams**, <http://books.google.com/ngrams>
- **OpenSubtitles 2018** via OPUS, <http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php>, which comes from <http://www.opensubtitles.org/>
- **Wikipedia**, <https://www.wikipedia.org>

### Unicode Han Database (character set, readings, meanings)

The character set (`kTGH`, the 2013 Table of General Standard Chinese Characters, level 1), readings (`kTGHZ2013`, `kMandarin`) and English glosses (`kDefinition`) come from the Unicode Han Database (Unihan), <https://www.unicode.org/charts/unihan.html>. They are used under the following license:
